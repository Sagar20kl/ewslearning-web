## 2. Exact Unit Economics & Real-World Pricing

### COST #1: AWS S3 Media Storage & Video Delivery (AWS CloudFront CDN)
* **Storage Cost**: AWS S3 Standard costs **$0.023 / GB / month (≈ ₹1.90 per GB / month)**.
  * **1 Full Video Course** (5 modules, 1080p compressed) = ~500 MB (0.5 GB).
  * **10 Full Video Courses** (5 GB) = **₹9.50 / month (literally under 10 rupees!)**.
  * **50 Full Courses + 500 PDF Toolkits** (30 GB) = **₹57 / month ($0.69/mo)**.
* **Video Delivery / Streaming (CloudFront CDN)**:
  * AWS gives **1,000 GB (1 Terabyte) FREE data transfer every single month**.
  * 1 TB allows **~2,000 full course video viewings every month at ₹0 bandwidth cost**!
* **Real Monthly S3 Cost: ₹10 to ₹60 / month ($0.12 to $0.75 / mo).**

### COST #2: Google Gemini AI API (Autonomous Blogs & Course Creation)

#### A. Weekly Automated Sustainability News Blog
* Fetching RSS news wires + generating a 2,000-word comprehensive compliance article with source citations via **Gemini 1.5 Flash**.
* **Cost per blog**: **$0.0009 (≈ ₹0.07 / blog — literally 7 paise!)**.
* **4 weekly blogs every month**: 4 × ₹0.07 = **₹0.28 / month (under 30 paise per month!)**.
* *(Note: Under Gemini's Free Tier with 15 requests/min, this is 100% FREE (₹0)!)*

#### B. AI PDF-to-Video Course Generator Studio
* Deconstructing a 50-page technical manual (e.g. CPCB Hazardous Waste Rules) into 3 modules, 5 video lesson scripts, and quiz questions.
* **Cost per full course generated**: **~₹0.85 (under ₹1 rupee per course!)**.
* **Generating 10 new complete video courses every month**: **₹8.50 / month**.

#### C. EcoMentor 24/7 AI Regulatory Copilot
* Answering 1,000 student & corporate employee compliance questions a month = **~₹15.00 / month** (or ₹0 on free tier).
* **Real Monthly Gemini AI Cost: ₹0 to ₹30 / month ($0 to $0.40 / mo).**
